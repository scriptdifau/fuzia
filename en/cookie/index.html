Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites save on the device of their visitors to remember information or to analyse how the site is used. They are divided into technical cookies (necessary for the site to work), analytics cookies and profiling cookies: for the last two types, unless they are anonymised, the law requires prior consent.

What this website uses

This website does not install cookies, neither its own nor third-party ones, and does not use other storage tools on your device (such as local storage). It does not use profiling cookies. For visit statistics it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and without storing information on your device (see below); for this reason it does not show a consent banner. The typefaces are hosted on the site itself and the site does not embed maps, videos or other third-party content that install cookies.

Visit statistics

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.) to know in aggregate the number of visits and the most viewed pages. The tool does not use cookies, local storage or other identifiers, and does not track visitors across different sites. More information: cloudflare.com/web-analytics. Details on the processing are in the Privacy Policy.

Restricted area

A part of the website, reserved to the controller and to the people they authorise to update the content, uses technical authentication tools. These do not affect visitors to the site.

Links to third-party sites

The website contains a link to the controller’s Instagram profile. When you open it you leave this site and that service’s privacy and cookie policies apply, for which the controller is not responsible.

If something changes in the future

If other tools or services that install cookies or store data on your device are introduced, this page will be updated before they are used and, where the law requires it, your consent will be requested.

How to control cookies in your browser

You can always view, block or delete cookies from your browser settings.
