Privacy notice

Information on the processing of personal data under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”). Last updated: 10 November 2026.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Jammali Faouzia, corso Racconigi 10, 10138 Turin, Italy, email: info@fuziajammali.com (“the controller”).

2. What data we process, why and on what legal basis

Contact requests

The “Contact me” form asks for your name, email address, subject (optional) and message. The form does not send your data to a server of this website: when you press “Send”, your email program opens with a pre-filled message, which is then sent from your own account to info@fuziajammali.com. The controller therefore receives the data in their own mailbox. The same applies if you write directly to that address.

  • Purpose: to reply to your enquiry or contact request.
  • Legal basis: performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request and, for the rest, the controller’s legitimate interest in replying to the requests received (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Providing your data: optional; however, without your name, email address and message we cannot reply to you.

Browsing data

To run the website, the technical systems of the providers listed below may automatically record some data, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and the type of browser. This data is used to ensure the security and proper functioning of the website (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The controller does not use it to identify visitors or to profile them.

Content loaded from external services

The “Press” section loads its list of news items from an external service (see “Recipients of the data”): your browser contacts the service directly, which may record your IP address as described above.

3. Cookies and statistical analysis

The website does not install cookies or other identifiers on your device and does not carry out profiling. To measure visits in aggregate form it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a statistics service that does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not reconstruct the behaviour of individual visitors across different sites. The service processes technical data about the visit (page visited, referring site, browser and device type, country); the IP address is used only to derive that information and is not kept in a form that identifies the visitor.

  • Purpose: to know in aggregate how many people visit the site and which sections interest them most.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Since no cookies or other identifiers are installed on your device, prior consent is not required.

More information in the Cookie Policy.

4. Recipients of the data

Data is not disclosed or sold. It may be processed on behalf of the controller, as technical service providers or data processors, by:

  • the website hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, by GitHub, Inc.; this notice will be updated if the provider changes);
  • the provider of the service that manages the content of the “Press” section (Supabase);
  • the provider of the aggregate website statistics service (Cloudflare, Inc.);
  • the controller’s email service provider.

Data may be disclosed to public authorities where required by law.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers are based, or use systems, outside the European Economic Area (in particular in the United States). In such cases the transfer takes place on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or of standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep your data

Messages received are kept for as long as necessary to reply to you and to manage any relationship that arises from them, and in any case for no more than 24 months from the last contact, unless the law requires a longer period. Browsing and statistics data is kept by the providers for the time strictly necessary for the purposes stated.

7. Your rights

You can ask the controller for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and you can object to the processing (Articles 15-22 GDPR). To exercise your rights, write to info@fuziajammali.com: we will reply within one month. If you believe that the processing infringes the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

This notice may be updated over time; the date of the last update is shown at the top.
